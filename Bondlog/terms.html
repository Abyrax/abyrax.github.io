Using BondLog

BondLog is a personal journal for recording when you’ve been in touch, setting reminders, and viewing summaries based on what you log. Use it lawfully and respect other people’s privacy. Only record or share information you have the right to use. Do not use BondLog to harass anyone or to monitor someone without their permission. You keep any rights you have in the content you enter.

Insights reflect the interactions you record. Incomplete records can produce incomplete summaries. Insights are for personal reference. They cannot tell you what another person feels or intends. Reminder delivery depends on your device and notification settings.

Your journal and backups

BondLog works without an account and stores the journal on your device. We cannot recover your local journal for you. Save an export somewhere you can access if you plan to change devices, delete the app, or use Delete All My Data. You can restore a compatible backup to add missing records while keeping your current data. Exported files are readable without a BondLog password. Keep them private. Privacy Policy
