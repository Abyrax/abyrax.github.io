Getting started

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Open BondLog and start a journal without signing up. People, logging, reminders, and Insights work offline. An internet connection is used for App Store services and when you open an external link, send support email, or choose an online file location.

What devices and accessibility settings does BondLog support?

BondLog requires iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 or later. It supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion. Use Settings → Preferences to change the app’s appearance or open system language settings.

Will someone know I added them?

BondLog does not contact people you add or read your address book, messages, or call history. A person’s entry comes from what you add yourself. See the Privacy Policy for details about on-device storage and what can appear in reminders and widgets.

How do I log or correct an interaction?

In the People list, tap someone to open their swipe panel. Slide right if you reached out or left if they did, then select In Person, Call, Message, or Video. Tap Undo immediately to reverse the log. To correct an older entry, open the person’s profile, choose Summary, and select the entry in Recent History.

Where did a person go?

In People, choose All and check Archived. Choose Unarchive to show the person in your active list again.

What do my notes and Insights show?

A person’s profile holds a memory cue, dated notes, birthdays, and other special dates. Upcoming dates can appear in Reach out. Insights and General stats summarize patterns in what you logged. Use a person’s Summary to see their contact rhythm and history. The summaries are calculated on your device and describe your logged activity.

Reminders and widgets

A reminder didn’t arrive

In the app, open Settings → Reminders → Reminder settings. Check Notification status and send a test. If notifications are off, allow them in iOS Settings. If the test arrives, open that person’s Reminders and check their one-time reminder or keep-in-touch schedule. A dated item can still appear in Reach out when notification permission is off.

How can I keep names out of reminders?

Turn on Settings → Privacy → Hide names in reminders for generic text in upcoming personal alerts. Use iOS notification preview settings to control what shows on the Lock Screen. Check any BondLog widgets you’ve added separately, because names can appear in widget content.

How do I use a widget?

Add BondLog from the iOS widget gallery. The small Home Screen widget gives you a quick view of four people. On a medium or large widget, tap a person, choose Them or You, then choose the channel. You can undo the log from the widget. Lock Screen widgets show a person and contact recency.

Moving, restoring, or deleting data

How do I move my journal to another device?

  1. On your old device, open Settings → Your Data → Export My Data.
  2. Save the JSON file somewhere you can open on the new device. Check Last backup export in Settings.
  3. On the new device, open Settings → Your Data → Restore from Backup and choose that file. Restore adds missing records while keeping your current records. Check that your people and history appear before removing the old copy.

Exports can contain personal details, notes, and photos. BondLog does not encrypt the file. Keep it private. If you use Apple’s device backup or transfer tools, what transfers depends on your Apple settings and available backup. About Apple device backups

Can BondLog recover a lost journal?

BondLog has no account or online copy of your journal. Check for an exported BondLog backup or an Apple device backup. Restoring Plus from the App Store recovers paid access only.

How do I delete my journal?

In the app, open Settings → Your Data → Delete All My Data and confirm. This permanently clears your journal from that installation and removes BondLog’s pending and delivered notifications from Notification Center. Manage saved exports, Apple device backups, and App Store subscriptions separately.

Plus and billing

Is BondLog free?

The free journal includes up to five active people, two categories, logging, 30 days of history in the list, Basic Insights, widgets, and backup tools. Optional Plus adds features including expanded history and Insights, smart reminders, and unlimited people and categories. Choose a monthly or annual subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Terms of Use

My Plus access is missing

Open Settings → BondLog Plus → Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you used to buy Plus. Check your plan on that screen. If your journal is missing too, follow the backup steps above.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Manage a Monthly or Annual subscription in Settings → BondLog Plus → Manage Subscription or in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting BondLog does not stop billing. Apple may charge for renewal during the 24 hours before the next period starts. Cancellation and refund options depend on your App Store country or region and applicable law.
