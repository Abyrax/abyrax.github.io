Questions, answered plainly.

Does BondLog read my messages or contacts?

No. BondLog can’t see your messages, contacts, or call history. You log each interaction yourself, including videos you sent or received.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in the app’s local database: people, photos, interactions, reminders, notes, dates, and insights. There’s no account and no relationship-data cloud. Apple handles purchases through the App Store.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

The journal works offline without an account. App Store services and links to external pages use an internet connection.

Is it free?

The journal is free to use, with no ads: up to five active people, two categories, unlimited logging, 30-day history in the list, and day/week plus one-time reminders.

An optional Plus upgrade adds unlimited people, categories, and history; 90-day, one-year, and all-time Contact rhythm with a typical-gap chart; period comparisons and channel changes; Plus Insights; smart reminders; minute/hour Keep in Touch reminders; and a Weekly review in Reach out. Plus is available as a monthly or annual subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. You can manage subscriptions and restore purchases in Settings. Basic Insights, logging, widgets and backup recovery stay free. Restoring a backup or unarchiving people remains available even above the free active-person allowance.

How do reminders work?

Each person can have a one-time reminder — set with hour and minute wheels or a custom date and time, with an optional note — and a separate keep-in-touch schedule that repeats weekly to custom. When something is due, Remind Me Later offers quick choices from 15 minutes to next week (or a custom date). Reminders are local notifications with Log, Snooze, and Complete actions, plus an optional weekly check-in on the day and time you choose. Reminder settings shows notification status and can send a test notification, and Hide names in reminders keeps names, notes, and occasion labels out of alerts.

What do the insights show?

Insights has two views. The feed ranks on-device observations — streaks, longest gaps, favorite dayparts, quiet channels, and category trends. A photo row filters the feed by person. General stats shows 7 days, 30 days, or all time, with interactions, people contacted, who reached out, and a communication breakdown by channel. Basic Insights is free; Plus adds typical-gap and two-period channel comparisons that open the matching Summary, with one matching preview free. New cards can be hidden with Undo and shown again, and you choose which people and types appear. Each person also keeps their own insights and summary. Calculated on device, and never about anyone’s feelings.

What are Special Dates, memory cues, and notes?

A birthday, labeled special dates, and a short memory cue you want to remember before reaching out. Birthdays and special dates appear in Reach out when they’re near, and the memory cue appears in their profile and in Reach out. Each profile also keeps a list of dated notes — add, edit, or delete them there, and saved notes appear on the person’s People card.

Can I log without opening the app?

Yes. The free medium and large Home Screen widgets let you tap a person, choose Them or You, then the channel, and the interaction is saved with Undo. It pages through up to twelve people, ordered by your list or by most overdue. The small widget shows a read-only grid.

Can I back up and restore my journal?

Yes, and it’s free. Export My Data creates a JSON backup including photos, notes, reminders, dates, interactions, and Insights state, and Settings records the date of your last successful export. Restore from Backup adds missing records from a file you choose without replacing current data. Recovery stays free, including when your restored active list goes above five people. Backups aren’t encrypted — keep them private.

What if something isn’t working?

Open Settings → Support → Troubleshooting for offline answers about a missing reminder, missing Plus access, moving to a new device, a person you can’t find, and an interaction logged by mistake. Reminder settings can also check notification status and send a test.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later. The app is localized in 28 languages, follows your system appearance with a System / Light / Dark picker, and includes a Language row in Settings. It supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion. The Support footer in Settings shows the version and build.
