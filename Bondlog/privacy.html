Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Ahmet Burak Yuksel operates BondLog as an individual and is responsible for the personal information handled through the BondLog website and support email. In this policy, “we” refers to Ahmet Burak Yuksel.

BondLog is a private relationship journal. You can use it offline without signing up. BondLog stores your journal on your iPhone or iPad and does not collect it, upload it to a BondLog server, or sync it between devices. The app has no ads or tracking and does not send usage analytics to BondLog.

What BondLog stores on your device

BondLog stores the people and categories you create, their names and optional avatars, notes and memory cues, birthdays and other important dates, reminders, and the date, channel, and initiator of interactions you log. It also stores app preferences and the local results and display state of Insights. Insights are calculated from your journal on your device.

You enter people and interactions yourself. BondLog does not read your address book, messages, call history, calendar, or location, and it does not notify anyone you add. If you choose an avatar photo using Apple’s photo picker, BondLog receives the selected photo and saves a compressed copy on your device.

What can appear outside the app

BondLog schedules reminders on your device. You choose whether to allow notifications and can change that permission in iOS Settings. With notifications enabled, names, reminder notes, or occasion labels can appear in notifications. Settings → Privacy → Hide names in reminders uses generic text for upcoming personal reminder alerts. If you add a BondLog widget, names and, depending on the widget, photos or contact recency can appear on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. Review your widget placement and iOS notification preview settings if other people can see those screens.

Backups, exports, and deletion

  • Export My Data creates a JSON file containing your journal, including selected avatar photos, notes, dates, interactions, and per-person reminders. The app records the date of the last successful export on your device. You choose where to save or share the file. BondLog does not encrypt the exported file.
  • Restore from Backup reads the file you choose and adds missing records while preserving current records. The file is processed on your device.
  • Apple’s device backups, including iCloud Backup, may include app data according to your Apple settings. An export saved to iCloud Drive or another provider is stored by the provider you choose. What iCloud Backup includes
  • Delete All My Data removes the journal from the app on this device, cancels pending BondLog notifications, and clears delivered BondLog notifications from Notification Center. BondLog cannot recover the local journal for you. Exports, device backups, and Apple purchase records are managed separately.

App Store purchases and Apple diagnostics

Apple handles payment, billing, and refunds. BondLog uses StoreKit to show offers, verify Plus access, and restore purchases on your device. BondLog does not receive payment card details or send your purchase history to its own servers. Apple’s App Store privacy information

Depending on your Apple settings, Apple may share aggregated or non-identifying app usage or crash information with developers. You can control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your device. Apple’s App Analytics privacy information

Website and support email

The BondLog website stores your theme preference in your browser and does not use advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs website visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub describes its use and retention of this data in its privacy statement. The site has no account or journal upload form.

If you email support, your message is routed through Zoho Mail. We receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and resolve related issues. Include only what we need to help. Leave out your journal, backup files, and other people’s personal details.

We remove routine support correspondence from the active mailbox within 90 days after the request is closed. We may retain correspondence longer when required by law or needed for a specific dispute or legal claim. Zoho Mail may retain deleted copies for recovery or according to configured archive settings. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, we process support email to provide assistance requested under our agreement with you or for our legitimate interest in answering questions and resolving issues. We may keep records to meet legal obligations or protect legal claims. GitHub Pages logs IP addresses for website security. GitHub and Zoho may process information outside your country.

Email contact@abyrax.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a copy in a portable format, ask about international transfers, or complain to your data protection authority.

Children

BondLog is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
